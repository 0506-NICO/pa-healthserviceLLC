Who We Are

Holistic Care, Rooted in Compassion

We believe true health includes the body, the mind, the spirit and the community around us — so we bring care for all of them under one roof.

Our 24 services span psychotherapy and psychological evaluation, physical rehabilitation and orthotics, non-invasive pain management, chronic disease and nutrition support, residential home care, Christian counseling and chaplaincy, public health consulting, French–English translation and recruitment. Whatever you need, we meet you with dignity and respect.
