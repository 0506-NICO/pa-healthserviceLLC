Who We Are

Holistic Care, Rooted in Compassion

P&A Health Services LLC believes true health includes the body, the mind, the spirit and the community around us. We bring these together under one caring team.

From home care and behavioral wellness to faith-based counseling, non-invasive pain support and public health services, we help individuals and families find the support they need — with dignity and respect. Our home care services are provided in partnership with Awesome Healthcare Solutions LLC, a Maryland-licensed Residential Service Agency.
