Nutrition

Small Food Swaps That Support Better Blood Sugar

You don’t need a complete diet overhaul to make a difference. Start with a few simple swaps.

  • Fill half your plate with vegetables. Non-starchy vegetables add fiber and nutrients with fewer calories.
  • Choose whole grains. Brown rice, oats and whole-wheat bread digest more slowly than refined grains.
  • Drink water first. Swap sugary drinks for water or unsweetened tea.
  • Pair carbs with protein. Adding beans, eggs, fish or nuts helps keep you full and steady.

This article is for general information only and is not medical advice. Talk with your healthcare provider about your specific situation.

Mind & Spirit

Five Everyday Ways to Ease Stress and Anxiety

Stress is part of life, but it doesn’t have to run your life. These habits can help.

  • Breathe slowly. Inhale for four counts, hold for four, exhale for six. Repeat a few times.
  • Move your body. A short walk releases tension and improves mood.
  • Limit the noise. Set boundaries on news and social media, especially before bed.
  • Pray, reflect or journal. Writing or praying through worries can bring perspective and peace.
  • Reach out. Talking with a counselor, pastor or trusted friend is a sign of strength.

This article is for general information only and is not medical advice. Talk with your healthcare provider about your specific situation.

Pain & Mobility

Living Well With Chronic Pain: Gentle, Non-Invasive Habits

Chronic pain touches every part of life. These everyday habits can complement your care plan.

  • Keep moving gently. With your provider’s approval, light stretching or walking can reduce stiffness.
  • Mind your posture. Small adjustments at your desk, bed and car can ease strain on joints.
  • Rest well. A regular sleep schedule supports your body’s ability to cope.
  • Track your patterns. A simple pain journal helps you and your provider see what works.

This article is for general information only and is not medical advice. Talk with your healthcare provider about your specific situation.
